Flipper Messenger

Compose, send, receive, and reply to carrier SMS from a user-owned Flipper Zero through an authorized Android phone.

How it works

The Flipper has no cellular modem. The Android companion performs real carrier SMS operations after bonded Bluetooth setup and a physical authorization step. The connection uses authenticated encryption, replay rejection, and duplicate-send protection.

Current versions: Android 0.0.45 and Flipper application 0.0.10.

Requirements

An Android 8.0 or newer phone with SMS service, a Flipper Zero running official firmware 1.4.3 or newer, and a microSD card are required. Carrier messaging charges may apply.
